# Roadmap

The Minnow roadmap is a separate page in this wiki: open the **?** menu and choose **Roadmap**, or search "roadmap".

It covers what has shipped, the direction the product is steering in, and which apps stay behind the release gate — without delivery dates.

Two things you may be looking for:

- **Why an app is missing from your dock.** Compare, Benchmarking, Experts, Calendar, and Email are gated. See [Apps overview](../apps/overview.md#not-in-this-release).
- **Where to file a request.** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/DukkyGames/Minnow/issues).
